## Learning Activities | Mixed ANOVA

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain how mixed ANOVA evaluates differences between groups and changes within participants, including whether change varies across groups.
- Interpret between-subjects effects, within-subjects effects, and their interaction in the context of a study.
- Evaluate the strength and limits of mixed ANOVA findings, considering how the design and pattern of results shape conclusions.

### Learning Sequence

1. Use statistical software to obtain the descriptive statistics, the source table, and the effect size associated with a Mixed ANOVA.
2. On your own, calculate the relevant elements of the source tables and eta-squared using the descriptive statistics given by the software. Verify that your calculations match the statistics provided by the software.Verify that your calculations match the statistics provided by the software.
3. Using appropriate APA Style, report your results from this set of analyses.

### Discussion

- What changes to the design, such as adding measurement occasions or groups, would help characterize when and for whom an outcome changes?
- How could missing repeated observations or differences in baseline scores affect the analysis and its interpretation?
- What additional contrasts or visualizations would you plan if a future study found evidence that change differs by group?
