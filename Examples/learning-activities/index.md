## Learning Activities: Instructor Overview

These instructor resources organize each procedure around learning outcomes, a guided learning sequence, and discussion prompts. They complement the worked examples without duplicating their calculations.

### Using the Learning Activities

Each activity connects a procedure to its purpose, calculation, and interpretation.

- **Learning Outcomes** states what students should understand and what instructors should emphasize.
- **Learning Sequence** connects the research question and data structure to the relevant calculation and interpretation.
- **Discussion** helps students examine what the result supports and consider its limits.
