## Learning Activities | One-Way ANOVA

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain how one-way ANOVA evaluates differences among the means of independent groups on a quantitative outcome.
- Interpret the omnibus result as evidence about whether group means differ, without assuming it identifies which groups differ.
- Evaluate the strength and limits of one-way ANOVA findings and identify when further comparisons or study are warranted.

### Learning Sequence

1. Use statistical software to obtain the descriptive statistics, the confidence intervals for the groups means, and the ANOVA source table.
2. On your own, calculate the elements of the source table and eta-squared using the descriptive statistics given by the software. Verify that your calculations match the statistics provided by the software.
3. Using appropriate APA Style, report your results from this set of analyses.

### Discussion

- How would you choose and justify a follow-up method for a future study with planned comparisons or unequal variances?
- What additional evidence would help determine whether the observed group-mean differences are practically important?
- How might larger samples, unequal group sizes, or violations of ANOVA assumptions change the study's conclusions?
