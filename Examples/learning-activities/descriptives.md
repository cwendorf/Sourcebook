# [Examples Articles](../index.md)

## Learning Activities | Descriptives

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain how numerical summaries and frequency distributions provide complementary perspectives on a set of observed scores.
- Interpret descriptive summaries in the context of the outcome and the distribution, recognizing what each can and cannot convey.
- Evaluate whether a summary gives a representative account of the data or whether additional descriptions are needed.

### Learning Sequence

1. Use statistical software to obtain a frequency distribution and estimates of the population mean, variance, and standard deviation for the variable.
2. On your own, calculate the mean, variance, and standard deviation using the information given to you in the frequency distribution. Verify that your calculations match the those provided by the software.
3. Using appropriate APA Style, report your results from this set of analyses.

### Discussion

- What features of a distribution would make the mean and standard deviation incomplete or misleading summaries?
- How could two samples with the same mean and standard deviation have different frequency patterns?
- Which additional summaries or visual displays would help evaluate whether a mean is representative of a future sample?
