## Learning Activities | Independent-Samples *t* Test

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain how an independent-samples comparison evaluates differences between separate groups on a quantitative outcome.
- Interpret the direction and magnitude of a group difference in the context of the study.
- Evaluate the strength and limits of evidence for a group difference and its relevance beyond the sample.

### Learning Sequence

- Identify the two independent groups, quantitative outcome, and order of the comparison.
- Locate the sample size, mean, and standard deviation for each group; describe the observed difference between group means.
- Calculate or verify the hypothesis test for the mean difference, including the test statistic, degrees of freedom, and *p* value.
- Calculate or locate the confidence interval for the mean difference and Cohen's *d*. Reversing the group order reverses their signs; software may use different comparison labels and may not report the effect size.
- Summarize the group descriptives, mean difference, test, confidence interval, and effect size in APA style.

### Discussion

- How would choosing and reporting a different comparison order change the description of the result without changing its substantive conclusion?
- What additional participants or a more representative sample would help make the estimate more precise or applicable to a broader population?
- What features of the study would you examine before interpreting a group difference as an effect of group membership?
