## Learning Activities | Frequencies

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain how frequencies and cumulative distributions describe the pattern of observed values.
- Interpret measures of relative position as ways to describe where observations fall within an ordered distribution.
- Evaluate how differences in percentile conventions can affect comparisons and interpretations across software or studies.

### Learning Sequence

1. Use statistical software to obtain a frequency distribution, median, and quartiles for the variable.
2. On your own, calculate the median and the quartiles using the frequency distribution given by the software. Verify that your calculations match those provided by the software.
3. Using appropriate APA Style, report your results from this set of analyses.

### Discussion

- How would the interpretation of the 25th-to-75th-percentile spread change depending on which software convention is used?
- What details about a percentile method would you include when comparing results across studies or programs?
- What does a frequency table reveal about observed scores, and what questions about the underlying measure remain unanswered?
